Quick help

  • Allow microphone access in iOS Settings if SingSeek cannot listen.
  • Hum a recognizable chorus steadily for 4–12 seconds in a quiet place.
  • Use Restore Purchases in SingSeek Settings after reinstalling or changing devices.
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple account subscription settings.
  • Delete your account and saved data from SingSeek Settings.
