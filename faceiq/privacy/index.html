Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-01-10

FaceIQ (“we”, “us”) analyzes facial features to provide personalized improvement tips. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your options. We take your privacy seriously, especially regarding biometric and facial data.

1. Introduction & Data Collection

We collect the following types of data to provide our service:

  • Personal Information: Email address (if creating an account).
  • Facial Data: Photos of your face uploaded for analysis.
  • Usage Data: App interactions, crash logs, and performance data.
  • Payment Data: Processed securely by Apple (StoreKit) and RevenueCat. We do not store credit card numbers.

2. Facial Data Policy

Because we process images of your face, we adhere to strict privacy standards:

  • What is collected?: Images of the user's face.
  • How is it used?: To analyze facial features using AI and provide personalized improvement tips.
  • Is it shared?: Yes, images are securely transmitted to our cloud providers (Firebase, xAI) solely for the purpose of analysis.
  • Retention:
    • Opal Labs retention: We do not retain face images. Images are deleted from our Firebase Storage immediately after analysis is completed and results are returned.
    • Third-party processor retention (xAI/Grok): Images transmitted to xAI are processed to generate analysis results. xAI may retain submitted content for up to 30 days and may retain it longer if legally required or if flagged for security/safety/compliance review, consistent with xAI’s enterprise/API terms.
  • Disclosure: We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your facial data to outside parties.

3. Third-Party Services

We leverage the following third-party providers to power our app:

  • Google Firebase: For authentication, database, and secure cloud storage.
  • RevenueCat: For managing subscriptions and purchase history.
  • Google ML Kit: For on-device face detection.
  • xAI (Grok): For generating the AI analysis and text explanation (facial analysis feedback). xAI may retain data for up to 30 days per their terms.

4. User Rights (GDPR/CCPA Compliance)

  • Right to Delete: Users can delete their account and all associated data directly within the app Settings.
  • Right to Access: Users can request a copy of their data by contacting support.
