Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-01-13

FaceIQ (“we”, “us”) analyzes facial features to provide personalized improvement tips. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your options. We take your privacy seriously, especially regarding biometric and facial data.

1. Introduction & Data Collection

We collect the following types of data to provide our service:

  • Personal Information: Email address (if creating an account).
  • Facial Data: A still photo (JPEG) of your face that you capture with the camera or choose from your photo library and explicitly upload for analysis. We do not collect Face ID data, depth data, or any biometric templates.
  • Usage Data: App interactions, crash logs, and performance data.
  • Payment Data: Processed securely by Apple (StoreKit) and RevenueCat. We do not store credit card numbers.

2. Facial Data Policy

Because we process images of your face, we adhere to strict privacy standards:

  • What is collected?: A still face photo (JPEG) uploaded by the user from the camera or photo library.
  • How is it used?: To analyze facial features using AI and provide personalized improvement tips.
  • Is it shared?: Yes, images are securely transmitted to our cloud providers (Firebase, xAI) solely for the purpose of analysis.
  • Storage (Opal Labs/Firebase): The image is uploaded directly to a private Firebase Storage path uploads/{uid}/{timestamp}.jpg in our Firebase project (region: us-central1) for processing.
  • Retention:
    • Retention (Opal Labs): We do not retain face images. The image is deleted from Firebase Storage immediately after analysis completes or fails (deletion occurs in the backend function after processing).
    • Retention (xAI/Grok): The image is transmitted to xAI for analysis. Per xAI enterprise/API terms, submitted content may be retained for up to 30 days and longer if legally required or flagged for security/safety/compliance review.
  • Analysis Results: The analysis score and tips are returned to the device for display and are not stored in our databases. We store only a minimal user_stats usage counter to enforce usage limits (no face images, no analysis text, no scores).
  • xAI processing details: Our backend sends the full uploaded image to xAI (Grok) for analysis. We do not send your user ID, email address, or other account identifiers to xAI.
  • Biometric Exclusion: We do not perform identity verification, face recognition, or matching against a database. We do not generate or store faceprints/embeddings/biometric templates.
  • User Control: Face images are uploaded only when you explicitly select/capture a photo and submit it for analysis.
  • Disclosure: We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your facial data to outside parties.

3. Third-Party Services

We leverage the following third-party providers to power our app:

  • Google Firebase: Temporary storage of uploaded face images for processing (deleted immediately after processing).
  • RevenueCat: Subscription status only; no face images.
  • Google ML Kit: On-device face detection only; images do not leave the device for ML Kit processing.
  • xAI (Grok): Receives the uploaded image for analysis feedback; may retain submitted content up to 30 days per their terms.
  • Crashlytics/Analytics: App diagnostics/metadata only; no face images.

4. User Rights (GDPR/CCPA Compliance)

  • Right to Delete: Users can delete their account and all associated data directly within the app Settings.
  • Right to Access: Users can request a copy of their data by contacting support.
