Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025-10-26

Velox (“we”, “us”) upscales short videos using cloud processing. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your options.

Data we collect

  • Account: email and Firebase user ID when you sign in.
  • Purchases: subscription status from RevenueCat (no full payment card data).
  • Jobs: metadata for your uploads and results (e.g., filenames, sizes, status, timestamps).
  • Content: uploaded videos and generated outputs stored to deliver results.
  • Diagnostics: crash/error logs (if enabled) to fix issues.

How we use data

  • Authenticate, process, and deliver video jobs; show history.
  • Validate subscriptions and restore access across devices.
  • Maintain security, performance, support, and prevent abuse.

Sharing

We use processors acting on our behalf, including Google Firebase (auth, database, storage), RevenueCat (subscriptions), and our video processing infrastructure. They process data under our instructions and their privacy terms.

Retention

We retain account, purchase, and job data while you use the app and as needed for legal, security, and accounting purposes. See “Delete your data”.

Your rights

You can request access, correction, portability, objection, or deletion where applicable to your region’s laws (e.g., GDPR/CCPA). We will verify your request via your account email.

Delete your data

  • In the app: Settings → Danger zone → Delete account. This deletes your jobs, profile document, storage artifacts, and Firebase Auth record.
  • By email: write to support@opallabsdev.com from your account email with subject “Velox account deletion”.

We aim to complete deletion within 7 days (no later than 30). Minimal logs/payment records may be retained for up to 90 days where required.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel an active App Store or Google Play subscription. Cancel in your store settings.

Children

The service is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your region).

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be noted by updating the date above.

Contact

Email: support@opallabsdev.com
